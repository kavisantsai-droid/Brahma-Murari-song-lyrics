Meaning of the Brahma Murari Song

Welcome back to our website, today we are going to explore the meaning of Brahma Murari song!

Meaning of the Song

Supreme Gods bow to Shiva
Represents purity and divine light
Worship removes Karmic suffering
Complete surrender to the supreme

Worshiped by Sages and Gods
Destroyer of desires, full of compassion
Destroyed Ravana's pride
Bow to eternal Shiva
